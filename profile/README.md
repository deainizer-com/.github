[DeAINizer](https://deainizer.com) is an online watermark-cleaning product for files you own or are authorized to edit.

Available now:

- Visible NotebookLM watermark cleaning for MP4 video exports
- Visible NotebookLM watermark cleaning for PDF exports
- Visible NotebookLM watermark cleaning for PPTX exports

Planned for later:

- Broader visible-text and AI-media watermark-cleaning workflows

Planned features are not available today. Results can contain localized reconstruction differences, especially where a visible mark overlaps detailed content.

DeAINizer is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or NotebookLM. It does not claim to remove SynthID or other invisible provenance signals.
